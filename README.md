# The Factors Behind Life

A detailed Regression Analysis project that goes into the life expectancy of health data.

# Introduction

"Life", a four-letter word that carries one of the heaviest meanings for all of us. People 
aspire to have a good life, a happy life, and often a long life. Since "life" is such an important 
topic, it is natural to ask whether we can predict a certain aspect of "life" through statistical 
means or not. Is this process possible? In this project, we explore this aspect of life through the 
lens of regression analysis. 

Our dataset contains socioeconomic, health-related, and demographic variables from 
countries around the world. Our goal is to examine life expectancy and investigate any 
interesting relationships between life expectancy and the variables from our dataset. 
Additionally, we hope to answer these questions for our project: Do the relationship between life 
expectancy and the selected variables make sense? From our regression model, what is the 
biggest factor influencing "life expectancy"? How accurate are our predictions from this 
regression model? By exploring these questions, we hope to gain insight into what shapes human 
longevity and better understand our "life".

# What did we do?

We checked for which variables we should use in our regression model equation and check the validity of our equation after by checking error assumptions using R programming.
